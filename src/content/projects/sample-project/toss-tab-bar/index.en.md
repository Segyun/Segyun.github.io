Recreates the fluid, elastic tab transition animations and delicate haptic feedback inspired by the Toss mobile application in SwiftUI.

Leverages `matchedGeometryEffect` combined with spring animation curves to deliver a stretchy, liquid-like indicator that glides between active tabs.

Fully customizable with custom icon sets, label typography, and brand accent colors while binding cleanly with standard SwiftUI state.
