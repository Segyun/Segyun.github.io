Implements responsive scroll interactions inspired by the Karrot (Daangn) home feed, where the top navigation bar collapses and expands based on scroll velocity and offset.

Activates frosted glassmorphism background pills behind search and notification buttons as the toolbar collapses, maintaining high legibility over scrolled content.

Features subtle micro-interactions where the bottom-right floating action button collapses its text label into a compact circular icon during active scrolling.
