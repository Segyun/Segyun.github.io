Simulates the soft radiance and atmospheric light diffusion of physical tube lights by combining angular and linear gradients in SwiftUI.

Provides flexible visual controls over glow expansion radius, luminosity intensity, and tube thickness.

Fully compatible with native SwiftUI `Color` assets, making it ideal for modern neon and ambient glow backgrounds and highlight accents.
