A fluid header interaction inspired by Apple Music, where the top image stretches smoothly in response to scroll gestures, while offering elegant blur and fade-out effects.

Easily integrated anywhere inside a `ScrollView` using either the `FluidHeader` container or the `.fluidHeader()` view modifier.

Supports intuitive customization of scroll sensitivity, blur radius, and fade offsets through environment-based view modifiers.
