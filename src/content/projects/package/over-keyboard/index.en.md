![[thumbnail.png]]

Monitors the distinct window hierarchy where the iOS system keyboard resides, allowing you to seamlessly position floating action bars or autocomplete toolbars directly above the keyboard.

Precisely tracks keyboard show and hide lifecycle animations to eliminate jitter and ensure fluid transitions.

Applies smart touch pass-through to transparent areas so background view interactions and keyboard inputs remain uninterrupted.
