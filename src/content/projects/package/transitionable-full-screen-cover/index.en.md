pubDate: 2025-12-26
githubUrl: "https://github.com/Segyun/TransitionableFullScreenCover"
---

Extends standard SwiftUI `.fullScreenCover` presentations by introducing customizable `AnyTransition` parameters to give full control over entrance and exit animations.

Allows combining slide, scale, and fade effects with fine-tuned spring timing curves to build tailored modal presentations matching your app's design language.

Inherits standard SwiftUI presentation syntax seamlessly to minimize refactoring overhead in existing codebases.
